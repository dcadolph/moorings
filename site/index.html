On an ultrawide

Pin the edges. Work in the middle.

Anchor up to four apps to the sides of the screen, a top and a bottom slot on each side. Everything else stays in the open middle, the channel. Nothing is anchored until you pick an app, so a first start moves no window.

Four slots

Two and one
One and none
Two and two

Anchor one app to a whole side, two to its top and bottom, or none, and the channel takes the space. Pick widths and heights from presets or the Adjust strips editor.

Green button

Lift Slack over the channel

Click an anchored app's green button to lift it over the channel, and again to lower it. A free window's green button fills the channel.

Let it go

Slack releasedClick its green button to put it back.

Drag an anchored window away and Moorings lets it go, with a message that says how to bring it back.

The channel

The middle stays clear.

A window that strays into a strip is pushed back. One that is too wide is narrowed, and it grows back when the channel widens. Native fullscreen fills the channel instead of covering your anchored apps.

Cast off this window lets one window cover the strips until it closes. Tidy channel, in Window > Organize, lines up the front windows, and Undo tidy puts everything back.

Snap by dragging

Drag to an edge. Let go.

Drag a window to an edge or a corner and a footprint shows where it will land. Let go and it snaps. Hold Option while dropping on an ultrawide strip and the app is anchored there.

Settings decides what each edge and corner does on landscape and portrait displays, which areas never snap, and which keys must be held. The footprint takes your color, opacity and fade, and a Force Touch trackpad can tap as it crosses into a new zone.

Custom areas send a window dropped on any part of a display other than the ultrawide to an action of your own. Turn on the snap panel and a pause mid-drag shows a row of targets above the pointer, each drawn as the part of the screen it fills.

Workspaces

Save a layout. Get it back.

Save where every window sits on every display under a name, then recall it from the menu, a shortcut or a link. Tie one to a set of displays and plugging into that desk recalls it.

Profiles go further. Save your shortcuts, snapping and behavior under a name with a group of workspaces, then switch the whole setup with one key. Anchored apps stay put.

A recall can open the apps it names that are not running and place their windows when they show. App rules send an app's first window to the action you pick, every time it opens.

Shortcuts

Every size, one key away.

Left and right halves that step through 1/2, 2/3 and 1/3, quarters, thirds, maximize, center and next display are bound from the first launch. Sixths, eighths, twelfths, sixteenths, one-axis resizes, moves to an edge or corner, and Arrow mode wait in the Window folder for a key of your choice. Every action is also a moorings:// link, so anything that opens a URL can run it, and Shortcuts can run it as Run Moorings Action.

Behavior

Press again and it keeps going.

Seven choices decide what a second press does, from stepping the size to crossing to the next display. Set them, and the rest, in Settings > Behavior.

Press again

1/2, then 2/3, then 1/3

Press a left or right half again to step its size. Or send it on to the next display, flip it to the other side, or walk a corner around the screen.

Drag it back

Back to its own size

Drag a snapped window away and it gets its earlier size back at once, still under your pointer.

To the point

Slack lands exactly

Apps such as Slack land on the exact frame. A title bar double-click can maximize and restore, and the pointer can follow the window it moved.

With the pointer

Hold a key and point.

Pick the keys in Settings > Behavior, hold them and move the pointer. Throw and hover act on the window under the pointer, focused or not, and a footprint shows where a throw will land. Both stay off until you pick their keys.

Throw

Right Option, then a nudge right

A short move toward a side or corner picks a half or a quarter. A longer one picks thirds, sixths, maximize or center. Let go to send the window there.

Grid

Drag across the cells you want

The Grid action lays cells over the window's display, inside the channel on an ultrawide. Drag across a span, or click one cell, and the window fills it. Arrow keys and Return work too.

Hover

No click needed

Hold the move keys and the window follows the pointer, or the resize keys to grow it from its nearest corner. Drag the edge two windows share and both resize.

Tile and stash

Windows that keep their places.

Tiling keeps the free windows of a display in a tall, wide, columns, rows or BSP layout as windows open and close, inside the channel on an ultrawide. Drag one away and it floats until you pick a layout again.

Tiling

Tall, wide, columns, rows, BSP

Grow or shrink the main window, and focus or swap the window to the left, right, above or below, with tiling on or off.

Stash

A sliver at the edge

Park a window at a screen edge with a sliver showing. Rest the pointer on it and it slides out. Move away and it slides back.

Undo move

Ten steps per window

Undo move and Redo move step a window back through its last ten moves and forward again, each window on its own.

Get moorings

It moves windows. Nothing else.

Moorings needs Accessibility access to move windows. No account, no server, no analytics. Every few hours it checks GitHub for a new release and shows it in the menu. To check right away, choose Check for updates now. Nothing installs on its own. Read the privacy policy.
